Model architecture

  • Backbone: ImageNet pre-trained VGG-16 (First 13 conv layers)
  • Backend: 6 Dilated convolutions (Rate = 2) for multi-scale context
  • Attention: Squeeze-and-Excitation channel recalibration
  • Output: 4 Dedicated Density Channels (Head, Turban, Veil, Cap)
  • Video Engine: Continuous frame-by-frame inference with live HUD overlays

Safety Zone Benchmarks

  • SAFE (≤ 50): Normal free-flowing movement
  • NORMAL (51–150): Dense gathering, monitor exits
  • CRITICAL (> 150): High congestion alert, deploy crowd control
